Welcome to the Danone Cybersecurity Criticality Assessment
This tool will help you classify your application based on data sensitivity, confidentiality, integrity, and availability impacts. The assessment takes approximately 10-15 minutes.

1. Data Sensitivity & Classification

Select the most sensitive data type handled by the application

2. Confidentiality Impact

⚠️
Scenario: All data in this application is disclosed to unauthorized parties (e.g., public leak, competitor access, ransomware exfiltration). What would be the WORST impact?

3. Integrity Impact

⚠️
Scenario: Critical data in this application is modified or corrupted (accidentally or maliciously). What would be the WORST impact on business operations, product safety, or financial systems?

Consider impacts on: Product Safety, Production (IT/OT), Quality, Financial Systems, Customer Trust

4. Availability Impact

⚠️
Scenario: This application becomes completely unavailable (e.g., ransomware, infrastructure failure, cyber attack). What would be the impact?

Consider: Recovery Time Objective (RTO), Business Process Impact, Financial Impact
